Company Introduction

Xiangyang Summer Arts & Crafts Co., Ltd. has over 15 years of experience in the home decor and lifestyle industry, with extensive expertise in product development, design trends, quality control, and global market requirements.

We specialize in home decor products, including vases, candles, candle holders, ceramic cups, plates, bowls, and other artistic lifestyle items.

Inspired by aesthetics, art, and modern living, we are committed to creating products that bring beauty, warmth, and elegance into everyday spaces. By combining creative design, fine craftsmanship, and practical functionality, we provide stylish and inspiring home decor solutions for customers worldwide.

With a passion for artistic living and a deep understanding of the home decor market, we continuously explore new designs and trends, delivering high-quality products that enhance modern lifestyles.
